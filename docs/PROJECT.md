## 13. Confidentiality & Public-Disclosure Risks

- **Proprietary Energy Monitoring Logic:** Energy flow calculations, private API schema structures, and proprietary algorithms used in production energy management apps cannot be exposed as raw code.
- **Client NDA Compliance:** Project case studies must focus on generalized technical architecture, system patterns, and sanitized code abstractions rather than proprietary company assets or internal tool naming.
- **Public Disclosure Strategy:**
  - Use generic, highly descriptive technical titles for case studies (e.g., *"Commercial Energy Management iOS & Android Mobile Application"* instead of proprietary brand names).
  - Create original, sanitized architectural diagrams for case studies to illustrate system design without exposing proprietary client IP.
